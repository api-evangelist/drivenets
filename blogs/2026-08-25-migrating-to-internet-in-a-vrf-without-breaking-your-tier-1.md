On a service provider backbone, that database is the Global Routing Table (GRT). Today, most backbones force two completely different trust domains to live in the same house. The GRT holds well over a million untrusted public internet prefixes right alongside the operator’s most critical infrastructure, its loopbacks, link addresses and control-plane endpoints.
